Spring Beats Concert

Friday, April 18 · 7:00 PM · Northbridge Amphitheater

Join the Student Activities Board for an unforgettable evening of live music, food trucks, and community energy. Spring Beats features student bands, local performers, and a closing set by Northbridge’s own indie ensemble.

About this event

Spring Beats is one of Northbridge University’s largest annual traditions. The concert brings together students, faculty, and local residents for a relaxed evening of music and connection. Doors open at 6:30 PM, and the first performance begins at 7:00 PM.

What to expect

  • Three live band performances and a student DJ set.
  • Food trucks with vegetarian, vegan, and halal options.
  • Seating on the lawn; blankets and folding chairs are welcome.
  • A quiet sensory area near the east entrance.

Schedule

  1. 6:30 PM — Doors open and food trucks begin service.
  2. 7:00 PM — Opening set: Northbridge Jazz Collective.
  3. 8:00 PM — Main set: The Quad Tones.
  4. 9:00 PM — Closing set and community encore.

Accessibility and participation

The amphitheater has step-free access from the south plaza. ASL interpretation is available for the main set. If you need accommodations, please contact access@northbridge.edu at least five days before the event.
