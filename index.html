About the guide

The Campus Event Guide is maintained by the Office of Student Engagement. It helps Northbridge students find activities that match their interests, schedules, and comfort levels.

Every listing includes practical details such as time, location, category, and accessibility notes so students can plan with confidence.

  • Weekly highlights of the most anticipated campus events.
  • Accessibility and participation information for each featured event.
  • Direct links to registration and organizer contact details.
